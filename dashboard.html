Transcript

Direction comes from the cap. The words come from this computer's or phone's microphone — the cap cannot hear words.

Recognising speech is your browser's job, and it may send audio over the internet to do it. A line reading “direction unclear” means the cap could not agree with itself while that was said — not that it was ignored.

How to connect

Connecting

  1. Switch the cap on. It runs its self-test, so you should feel front, right, back, then left.
  2. Press Connect device above.
  3. Pick EchoSense in the window your browser opens, then press Pair.
  4. The dot next to the button turns green and the live view starts moving.

What you need

  • Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, Mac or Linux. iPhone and iPad cannot do Web Bluetooth at all.
  • The page must be on https, or on localhost.
  • Bluetooth switched on, and the cap within a few metres.

If the cap doesn't appear

  • A cap talks to one device at a time. Close any other tab, phone or laptop that still holds the connection.
  • Power the cap off and on, then press Connect again within a few seconds.
  • If the browser window opens but stays empty, the cap is out of range or not advertising.

The cap does not need this page. It listens and buzzes on its own, with or without a phone nearby. This is only here to change settings and watch what it is doing.
